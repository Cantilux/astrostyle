Right to left

Every component follows the reading direction: set dir="rtl" on the page (or on any container) and layouts, chevrons, fills and motion mirror, with no extra class. The samples below sit in a dir="rtl" lang="ar" container. Keys follow the direction too: in a horizontal tablist, ← moves to the next tab.
