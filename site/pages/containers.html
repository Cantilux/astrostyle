Containers

Dialogs, drawers and accordions hold content that comes and goes; tables, lists, dividers, key caps and skeletons arrange and stand in for it. Dialogs and drawers are native <dialog> elements with a small JavaScript module; everything else is HTML and CSS.

Dialog 01

Close a dialog: ap:close reports its return value here.

Jump to sector 7G?

The drive needs two cycles to cool down afterwards. Press Escape, click outside or pick an answer.

Hull breach

A click outside does not close this one: answer, or press Escape.

 
<button class="ap-c-button" type="button" data-ap-dialog-open="jump">Jump</button>

<dialog class="ap-c-dialog" id="jump" aria-labelledby="jump-title" data-ap-dialog>
  <header class="ap-c-dialog__header">
    <h2 class="ap-c-dialog__title" id="jump-title">Jump to sector 7G?</h2>
  </header>
  <div class="ap-c-dialog__body">…</div>
  <footer class="ap-c-dialog__footer">
    <button class="ap-c-button" type="button" data-ap-dialog-close value="cancel">Cancel</button>
  </footer>
</dialog>
import { createDialog } from 'astrostyle/js/dialog'

const jump = createDialog(document.querySelector('#jump'), { dismissible: true })
jump.element.addEventListener('ap:close', (event) => console.log(event.detail.returnValue))

A native modal dialog: the page behind is inert and Escape closes it. The module adds openers (data-ap-dialog-open), close buttons whose value becomes the return value, backdrop close, focus return and the ap:open and ap:close events.

Accordion 03

How far can the ship jump?

Three sectors per cycle, two with a full hold.

Who can pilot it?

Anyone with a license and a steady hand.

What happens when the drive overheats during a jump that was already planned?

The jump is cancelled and the drive cools for one cycle.
