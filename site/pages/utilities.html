Truncate 02

ap-u-truncate keeps a single line and ends it with an ellipsis when the text is longer than its box, like this sentence that keeps going far beyond the edge.

Visually hidden 03

ap-u-visually-hidden hides content from the eyes but not from screen readers. ap-u-visually-hidden-focusable comes back while focused: press Tab from the top of this page to see the skip link.

Responsive display 04

Display utilities have mobile-first variants: ap-u-hidden@md hides from the md breakpoint up. Resize the window.
