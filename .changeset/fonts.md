Bundle the Design Guide fonts whose license allows redistribution (Feature Mono, Instrument Serif, Genesys, ST-Cyberillic) in `fonts/`, with the optional `astrostyle/fonts.css` and the `scss/fonts` module declaring them. Powerr, Rimma Sans and SCHABO get their faces from the project's own files through `$licensed-path`. The docs site now uses the bundled fonts.
